# Brand and naming

**Name.** GenInvestor, written as one word with a capital G and I. Not "Gen Investor" or "Gen-Investor".

**What it names.** The open-source project in this repository and the tools around it: [geninvestor-skills](https://github.com/frankxai/geninvestor-skills), and learning materials as they are published.

**Independence.** GenInvestor is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any company or product with a similar name.

**Marks.** This project has no registered trademark and no logo yet. The Apache-2.0 licence does not grant permission to use the project's name or marks except to describe where the work came from (section 6 of the licence). If you fork the code into a product, give your product its own name.

**If you believe the name conflicts with your rights,** open an issue on this repository. A rename is inexpensive while the project is small, and we would rather resolve it early.

**Voice.** Plain and exact. Sentence case. Numbers come with their sources. No hype words, no promises about returns, and never "buy this" or "sell that".

**What we will not do under this name.** Give personal investment advice, take custody of anyone's assets, place orders, rank brokers by what they pay us, or publish performance claims that are not backed by a scored, dated record.
