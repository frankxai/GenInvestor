# Manifesto

We are building tools for investors who would rather be right for the right reasons than confident for the wrong ones. It is education and tooling. It is not advice.

1. **Show your sources.** Every number opens to where it came from and when it was true.
2. **Say what you do not know.** Stale data, missing data and unchecked claims are reported, never hidden.
3. **Write down how you would be wrong,** before you have a stake in being right.
4. **Score your calls.** Forecasts are registered before the outcome, never edited, and shown with their sample size.
5. **Own your data.** Local first. Your holdings never need to leave your machine.
6. **Nothing moves money.** Tools inform. People decide, and people act.
7. **Teach, do not hype.** No promises of returns, no "picks", no rankings that follow the referral fee. The aim is to help people learn to research well. The results are theirs.
8. **Build in the open.** Take what works from others, credit it, give back, and say plainly when we borrowed.

If that sounds like the way you already work, or the way you want to, you are welcome here.
